          Protocol Mechanics & Safety Guide
          
How Aurora Works

          
Read this before forging vaults or paying attempt fees on DuskVM

        

        
      


      

        

          
1. 2-Step Commit–Reveal (Anti-Front-Running)

          

            If you submitted a plaintext answer in a single transaction, mempool bots could see your answer and steal the bounty before your transaction confirmed. Aurora prevents this with a 2-step flow:
          

          
    
            
  • Step 1 — Blinded Commit (commit_solution): Anchors a salted SHA-256 hash bound to your Moonlight BlsPublicKey. Nobody can see your guess.
    • 
            
  • Step 2 — Reveal & Claim (reveal_solution): At least 1 block later, reveal your plaintext answer + salt. The contract verifies your commitment, transfers the DUSK bounty immediately, and publishes the winning answer on-chain.
    • 
          

        


        

          
2. Attempt Fee Escrow (“Virtual PoW”)

          

            When forging a vault, the creator locks an initial DUSK Bounty and can set an Attempt Fee (up to 50% of the initial bounty) charged on each Step 1 commitment:
          

          
    
            
  • 20% of every Attempt Fee is added directly to the vault’s Bounty Jackpot for the winning solver.
    • 
            
  • 80% of every Attempt Fee is locked in Creator Fee Escrow—the creator only unlocks these fees if someone actually cracks the riddle.
    • 
          

        


        

          
3. How to Reclaim Funds When a Riddle Expires

          

            Every vault has a mandatory expiry duration (minimum 200 blocks) so funds can never be trapped forever:
          

          
    
            
  • Once Current Block >= Expiry Block (plus a 5-block reveal grace window for any last-second commitment), the vault badge turns Expired.
    • 
            
  • Open the vault in the Inspector and click “Settle Expired & Refund Fees” (calls reclaim_expired). Anyone—either the creator or any solver—can click this button.
    • 
            
  • That single transaction refunds the original bounty principal back to the creator and automatically refunds 100% of every solver’s attempt fees back to their Moonlight wallets.
    • 
          

        


        

          
⚠️ 4. Risk Warning: New or Unproven Riddlers

          

            Because riddles are written by humans, the riddle creator always knows the secret answer. On a permissionless blockchain, a dishonest creator could write an unsolvable or gibberish riddle, wait for players to pay attempt fees, and then solve their own riddle from a second wallet to capture the fee pool.
          

          
    
            
  • Check the Creator Track Record: Every vault displays the creator’s on-chain history of previously cracked vaults and their revealed answers (e.g. 2 solved ("silence", "witness") vs. New riddler (0 solved)).
    • 
            
  • Full Post-Solve Transparency: Whenever a vault is cracked, the contract forces the plaintext winning_answer to be published permanently on-chain, immediately exposing any creator who used a fake answer.
    • 
            
  • Play Smart: Avoid paying large attempt fees on vaults from brand-new addresses (0 solved) or riddles with vague, subjective prompts.
    • 
          

        


        

          
5. Leaderboard Privacy (Shadow Mode vs. Public Alias)

          

            When revealing a winning solution (or anytime afterward via On-Chain Privacy Control), you choose whether the contract exposes your BlsPublicKey + custom alias on the leaderboard, or masks your key on-chain as null behind a deterministic Shadow #xxxxxx tag.
